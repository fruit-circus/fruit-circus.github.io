Dream Anchor
My last tether to the Atrament. Heron taught me this on my last day in the palace before Devi and I returned to Earth. Now the three of us meet up about every week. I don't know exactly how it works, but Dream Anchor has a limited ability to make dream objects physical. Heron crafted the spell with a number of complicated safe-guards. If it weren't for those safe-guards I could accidentally dream things into reality on Earth, or be killed dreaming of events in the Atrament.

Real-time translation for Ziq’s fucked up weird English that I can kind of understand but sometimes can’t be bothered with.
